COMMANDER RULING — LOOP 030. Locked framing + sustained-vs-transient measurement.

Loop 029 ACCEPTED on every point except the eyeline max. Credit for honestly
reporting the QC FAIL instead of self-approving.

The Commander watched the render. Verdict: framing quality is good, but the
frame BOBS as the subjects move their heads. That is the defect to fix. A
locked frame reads as a tripod; a frame that chases movement reads as amateur.

=== RULING 1: LOCKED FRAMING IS THE DEFAULT ===

Stop tracking continuously. For each shot, SOLVE for a single STATIC crop box
that holds every framing band across the entire shot, then do not move.

All values named in presets/framing/magnolia_interview.yaml, zero literals:

a) For each shot and each panel, sweep candidate static crops over crop_y,
   crop_x and crop height. Evaluate EVERY frame of the shot against all framing
   gates using the existing pixel-measurement engine.

b) Objective, in priority order:
   1. ZERO frames clipping the top of the head or breaching back-of-head margin
      (hard gates, no tolerance, any duration).
   2. Maximize frames with headroom and eyeline inside band.
   3. Tie-break: place the shot MEAN closest to target (headroom 0.08, eyeline 0.33).

c) A WIDER CROP IS THE PREFERRED WAY TO ACHIEVE A LOCK. Allow crop height to
   grow up to max_crop_height_for_lock (add to preset) to find a static
   solution. A slightly smaller head with a dead-still frame beats a larger head
   with a bobbing frame. Report the chosen crop height and resulting head size.

d) If a static crop satisfies the hard gates and keeps >=
   locked_frame_in_band_fraction (preset, 0.90) of frames inside the soft bands,
   LOCK IT. movement = 0, hold_fraction = 1.0 reported honestly.

e) ONLY if no static crop can satisfy the hard gates may the crop move. Then:
   - a move is permitted only after the subject is out of band longer than
     reposition_after_frames (preset, 45 = 1.5s) — never for a nod
   - each move is a single eased transition to a NEW STATIC HOLD, with
     min_hold_frames between moves
   - report the number of moves and the measured reason for each
   Continuous per-frame tracking is banned.

=== RULING 2: SUSTAINED vs TRANSIENT MEASUREMENT ===

With a locked frame the subject moves inside the frame, so brief excursions are
expected and are not defects. An instantaneous per-frame value cannot tell
"badly framed" from "person moved for a moment". Fix the measurement:

a) Evaluate eyeline and headroom on a ROLLING MEDIAN over
   sustained_window_frames: 15 (0.5s). The rolling median must stay inside the
   band. A sustained breach is a FAIL. This is the primary gate.

b) Bounded transient allowance — an exception, not a waiver. Exceeding ANY of
   these is a FAIL:
   - transient_max_fraction: 0.45   (instantaneous ceiling)
   - transient_max_frames: 45       (no single excursion longer than 1.5s)
   - transient_total_fraction: 0.10 (no more than 10% of a shot's frames outside band)

c) Do NOT apply this to head clipping or back-of-head margin. Those stay hard
   per-frame gates with zero tolerance — a clipped skull is a defect at any
   duration.
