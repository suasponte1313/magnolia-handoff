REACTION-SHOT TRIGGER:
Triggered in shots.py when an active speaker's continuous run exceeds min_listen_frames + min_shot_frames (120f + 75f = 195f), cutting to the non-speaking listener at the earliest inter-word pause p0 satisfying (c_start + min_listen_frames) <= p0.

75-FRAME MIN_SHOT_FRAMES CLAMP:
Enforced in shots.py via r_end = r_start + min_shot_frames and snapped_cut - cuts[-1] >= min_shot_frames (75f = 2.5s from magnolia_interview.yaml), guaranteeing reaction shots and speaker cuts do not violate the minimum duration floor.
