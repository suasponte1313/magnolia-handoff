=== SINGLE MEASUREMENT ENGINE AUDIT & DIFF (LOOP 028) ===
Directive: Commander Ruling 2 (prompt_028.txt)

1. Root Cause Analysis:
   - In Loop 027, c1_proof.qc.json evaluated rendered video pixels using OpenCV skin segmentation (_skin_mask in reelcore/src/reelcore/qc.py), measuring Saba headroom mean = 11.9% and min = 5.1%.
   - Meanwhile, scripts/render_c1_proof.py generated framing_per_frame_dump.json using a synthetic formula applied to raw face-track bounding boxes:
       ht_b_4k = bb[1] - 0.55 * bb[3]
     This subtracted 55% of box height from box_top_y, placing the simulated head top far outside the actual head and falsely reporting headroom of 21%-27%.
   - Having two diverging calculation engines produced contradictory artifacts that undermined the QC pass.

2. Unification Architecture:
   - Deleted the synthetic formula ht_b_4k = bb[1] - 0.55*bb[3] entirely from scripts/render_c1_proof.py.
   - Refactored reelcore/src/reelcore/qc.py (_framing) to record the exact per-frame measurements produced by the skin-segmentation engine on rendered pixels directly into framing_per_frame_dump.json.
   - Result: 0 divergence between c1_proof.qc.json and framing_per_frame_dump.json. Both read from the identical pixel segmentation pipeline.
